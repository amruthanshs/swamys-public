5. Why sauce

The biggest chicken brands in America are sauce brands

Raising Cane's sells one product and one sauce, and does $6.6M a store. Chick-fil-A's sauce is a cult object. Wingstop built a $4.8B system on flavors. Sauce is how Americans already try something new, and few cuisines have a deeper sauce tradition than Indian food.

At our tastings, people rated the sauces 8.4 out of 10, and 13 of 16 wanted them on the side, to dip and compare.

12. The economics

An $11.99 meal deal, built to typical fast-food margins

The deal sits right at the price fast-food customers start to call expensive, about $12. A $5 shake or chai upgrade lifts the ticket. At a typical fast-food cost structure, that leaves roughly $1.00–1.70 of profit per meal. Today, buying ingredients at retail for tastings, the chicken meal costs about $5.15 in food; restaurant-scale purchasing is how it gets to the target, and supplier quotes are next.
